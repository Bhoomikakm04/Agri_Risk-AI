Topic: Climate stress and heat
Crop: General
Risk: High temperature and climate stress
Guidance: Review local weather forecasts and crop-specific heat advisories. Monitor soil moisture and crop condition. Follow locally validated irrigation guidance and consult an agricultural extension officer when stress persists.

Document ID: AGRI-002
Topic: Water stress and irrigation
Crop: General
Risk: Low soil moisture or irregular rainfall
Guidance: Check soil moisture and the crop's growth stage before adjusting irrigation. Consider locally suitable water-efficient irrigation methods where appropriate. Avoid applying a fixed irrigation schedule without considering crop and soil conditions.
Source: Starter guidance for prototype testing; requires verification against an authoritative agricultural source.

Document ID: AGRI-003
Topic: Pest risk
Crop: General
Risk: Elevated pest risk
Guidance: Inspect crops regularly and identify the pest before choosing a control measure. Consider integrated pest management, field monitoring, and locally recommended preventive measures. Use pesticides only when appropriate and according to the product label and local guidance.
Source: Starter guidance for prototype testing; requires verification against an authoritative agricultural source.

Document ID: AGRI-004
Topic: Disease risk
Crop: General
Risk: Elevated disease risk
Guidance: Record visible symptoms, affected plant parts, crop stage, and recent weather conditions. Seek a reliable diagnosis before selecting treatment. Follow crop-specific recommendations from a qualified agricultural extension service.

Topic: General agricultural risk
Crop: General
Risk: Multiple risk indicators
Guidance: Review climate stress, pest risk, disease risk, soil moisture, and crop condition together. Prioritize field inspection and verified crop-specific advice instead of relying on a single risk score.
